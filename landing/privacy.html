Trace privacy

Updated September 29, 2026. This notice describes data used by Trace on Victory Road and in the Trace desktop app.

Account and sign-in

We use your email address and a unique account identifier to sign you in, connect your desktop app, and associate your membership with your account. Google sign-in requests basic identity information, including your email and profile. It does not request access to your Gmail inbox, messages, contacts, or Drive files.

Recorded matches

Trace records and stores captured match data, including player names, game actions, card information, results, and available ratings. Match data supports replay, training features, and leaderboard calculations. Access to private study features depends on your plan and match status.

Public results and sharing

Player names, results, and ratings can appear on Trace leaderboards. Creating a replay share link makes its permitted replay content available to people who have the link. Do not share a replay you want to keep private.

Payments and service providers

Stripe processes subscriptions and payment details. Trace stores membership and payment-reference information used to provide access. AWS and Vercel provide hosting, storage, authentication, and email infrastructure. Google processes Google sign-in when you choose that option.

Browser storage and saved history

Trace uses cookies to maintain authenticated sessions and pending sign-in or checkout steps. The desktop app stores captured matches locally and synchronizes supported data with Trace. The Free plan’s seven-day replay window is an access limit; it does not mean older saved matches are deleted.

Your choices and requests

You can turn off recording, sign out, unlink your app, and manage your subscription from Trace. For privacy questions or requests concerning account or match data, contact isaiah@quantumtalent.io. Include only the information needed to identify your request; never send passwords or sign-in codes.
