Good to know.

When can I see my opponent’s decklist?

Supporters Club members can view it after Trace has recorded that the match ended. It stays locked while a match is in progress, even if you close the game.

How do I connect my membership to Trace?

Create your account, choose a plan, then download Trace. In the app, select Link account. Sign in on Victory Road and confirm the code shown in Trace.

How do I change or cancel my plan?

Open My account and choose Manage billing. You can change your plan or cancel there. After canceling, your access continues until the end of your paid period.
