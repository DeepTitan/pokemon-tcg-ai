How do I get started?

Download Trace for your computer and open the installer. Follow the capture setup in the app, then play Pokémon TCG Live with Trace running.

Is Trace free?

Yes. Automatic recording, your last 30 days of replays, deck and prize study, leaderboards, and one new replay share every seven days are free. There’s no subscription required to get started.

Where do I link my account?

Choose Link account in Trace, then enter its code on the website. You can create a free account or sign in to your existing one.
